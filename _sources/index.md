## The latest

**[DITL 14 Oct, part 1](ditl-2026-10-14-part-1.md)** (15 Oct 2026): Over two days we priced a cycling stage race with a new model built on the day, prepared our F1 model for the US Grand Prix by fixing how flat its prices are, and checked our backtests against an independent full-depth copy of the 2026 F1 prediction-market order books from Probalytics. The tracked cycling slip lost all three bets (-$78.39 on $78.39, paper only), the F1 calibration improved accuracy and every paper strategy on two seasons of recorded prices, and the vendor data showed our one-price fills are about right on race-winner markets but 40% or more too cheap on thin ones. Part 2 follows in about two weeks, once the bets settle and our F1 and cycling strategies are locked in.
